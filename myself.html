Myself

Well, I'm Nikshep, a third-year student in IIT Madras, doing BS-MS dual degree in Physics. I'm from Chamarajanagara, Karnataka. There's honestly not much else to say about me (at least nothing that comes to mind right now).

I'm very much into Physics — which, given the existence of this webpage, probably isn't a surprise. I tend to go on a lot of random side quests, usually involving Relativity, Quantum Physics and Field Theories. Somehow, one question leads to another, and before I know it, I've wandered quite far from whatever I was supposed to be studying.

Outside Physics, I occasionally read philosophical literature, particularly texts inspired by Vedanta and Vachana literature, which I've found quite fascinating.

I also play the Hindustani semi-classical flute and spend a fair amount of time playing table tennis. So, yeah, that's pretty much me!
